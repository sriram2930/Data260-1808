Academic Notice
Undergraduate students are placed on academic notice if, at any time, their SJSU cumulative
GPA falls below 2.0. The CoSS SSC's mission is to provide support and guidance to
students on academic notice to help them return to good academic standing.
Students on academic notice will be placed in one of these three categories at the
end of the semester:
1. Good Academic Standing
The student has earned at least a 2.0 semester GPA, which is high enough to bring
the SJSU cumulative GPA to 2.0.
2. Continued Academic Notice
The student has earned at least a 2.0 semester GPA, but it is not high enough to bring
the SJSU cumulative GPA to 2.0. The student will be notified by a CoSS SSC Advisor
with next steps.
3. Disqualification
The student has earned below a 2.0 semester GPA (which will also bring the cumulative
GPA down). The student can request further information by going to the Disqualification
and Reinstatement page.

First Time Academic Notice
If this is your first time on Academic Notice, an Advisor will contact you to schedule
an appointment to discuss how to return to good academic standing.
Continued Academic Notice
If you are on Continued Academic Notice, an Advisor will contact you to schedule an
appointment to discuss how to return to good academic standing.
Students on Academic Notice within the College of Social Sciences will have a hold placed
on their account that will prevent them from making changes to their schedules. In
order to remove the hold, First Time and Continued Academic Notice students must complete
a set of specific requirements.
