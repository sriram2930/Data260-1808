Undergraduate Continuing Students who have not applied for graduation
Submit official transcripts via one of the following two methods:
1) Electronic transcripts (preferred method) should be sent to etranscript@sjsu.edu
directly from your school(s).
2) Send by mail to:
Checking your transfer credit status:
You will receive a confirmation email once the official transcript is received. The
notification will include the approximate time when your transfer credit will appear
on your transfer credit report on MySJSU (see below). Depending on the time of the
year, it usually takes at least 10 business days to process.
