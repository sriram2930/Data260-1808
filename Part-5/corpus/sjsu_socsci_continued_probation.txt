Continued Academic Notice
If you are on Continued Academic Notice* after Spring 2026, you will be invited to
participate in our Fall 2026 program that is in place to support you in returning
to good academic standing.
Communication will be sent to students soon after Fall 2026 semester begins. Please
monitor your preferred email for communication from us that will provide next steps.
*(SJSU Term GPA is above 2.0, but SJSU Cumulative GPA is below 2.0)
