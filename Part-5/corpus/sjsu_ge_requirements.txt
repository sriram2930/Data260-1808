General Education Requirements
The General Education (GE) requirements described on this page pertain to students
currently enrolled at SJSU.
California State University GE requirements will change in Fall 2025 to conform to
California Assembly Bill 928, the Student Transfer Achievement Reform Act, now known
as Cal-GETC: California General Education Transfer Curriculum.
First time first-year students starting in Fall 2025 or later will be required to
complete the new CSU GE Breadth Requirements. Most transfer students entering SJSU
in Fall 2025 or later will be placed into the new CSU GE Breadth Requirements.
Students are strongly encouraged to discuss their GE options with an advisor in their
college's Student Success Center
San Jose State requires students to take at least 43 semester units of general education
courses. (Multiply the semester unit requirement by 1.5 to calculate quarter-system
unit requirements.)
Students are expected to complete the general education requirements in Areas 1 and
2
(formerly A and B4)
before enrolling in any upper division
general education course.
